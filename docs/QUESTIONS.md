## from session 04 (2026-05-02)

1. **Has the wave-equation Huygens-tail signature (Log 03 / Project
   SLICE) been derived for compactified extra dimensions?** Specifically:
   if our universe is (3+1) macroscopic + 6 or 7 compact small dimensions,
   and a source emits in the bulk, what is the shape of the recorded
   tail in 3-space after integrating over the compact dimensions?
   This would distinguish "macroscopic 4th spatial dim" (clean
   1/t² tail) from "compactified extra dims at scale L" (modified
   tail with characteristic frequency c/L).

2. **Is the "factor-of-2 simultaneous violation" of classical and
   quantum 4D atom thresholds (Log 01) stated explicitly in the
   literature?** Tegmark 1997 cites the qualitative result; I don't
   know if anyone has flagged that the natural Bohr coupling
   *exactly* doubles both critical values via the same algebraic
   identity. Worth a literature dive.

3. **Should the 24-cell viz also include the action of left- and
   right-multiplication by a chosen quaternion?** That would
   demonstrate the SU(2)×SU(2) → SO(4) double cover *visually* —
   left q permutes vertices in one way, right q in another, and the
   product moves both. Could be a slider-driven viz like
   `viz/24cell_so4_action.html`.

4. **Are the cross-section polyhedra of the 24-cell (Exp 08) lattice
   polyhedra?** If they are, they should belong to the catalog of
   D_4-related lattice polyhedra, and there should be a closed-form
   for their vertex coordinates. Currently I generate them
   numerically.
